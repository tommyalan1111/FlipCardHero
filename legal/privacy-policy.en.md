## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1. Information you enter (stored on your device only)

The App lets you enter a **nickname / display name** (default "LEO") that is shown during a match.

- The nickname is stored in the App's local storage (WebView `localStorage`) on your own device.
- The nickname is **never transmitted to any server we operate**, is not used to identify you outside the game, and is not shared with third parties.
- You can change or clear the nickname at any time in the in-game settings, or delete all local data via **Android Settings → Apps → Flip Card Hero → Storage → Clear data**, or by uninstalling the App.

### 2.2. Information collected automatically by the advertising SDK (Google AdMob)

The App integrates the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** to display banner ads and full-screen (interstitial) ads. This SDK may automatically collect and process:

| Data type | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Device / advertising identifiers | Android Advertising ID (AAID), App Set ID | Ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency capping, measurement |
| IP address | IP used to connect to ad servers | Ad delivery, approximate (non-GPS) location at region level |
| Device & OS information | Device model, Android version, language, screen size, time zone, carrier | Serving ads appropriate to the device |
| Ad interactions | Impressions, clicks, view duration | Measuring ad performance and revenue |
| Diagnostics & performance | Error logs, SDK start-up timing, performance | Bug fixing and stability improvements |

This data is processed by **Google LLC** and/or Google's advertising partners as independent data controllers. We do not have access to that raw data and do not store it on our systems.

### 2.3. Device permissions

The App requests the following permissions, solely to operate game features and ads:

- `android.permission.INTERNET` — to load ads.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` — to check connectivity for ad loading.
- `android.permission.VIBRATE` — haptic feedback when you smash a card (game feature).

The App **does not** request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, messages, or personal files.

### 2.4. What we do NOT collect

We do not collect: full name, email address, phone number, postal address, precise GPS location, contacts, calendar, photos, audio files, messages, passwords, payment information (the App has no in-app purchases), health data, biometric data, or user-generated content shared publicly.

We also do **not** use any behavioral analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services beyond the advertising SDK described in Section 2.2.

## 7. ADVERTISING AND YOUR CHOICES

The App shows **banner ads** at the bottom of the screen and **full-screen (interstitial) ads** after a match ends.

You can control personalized advertising as follows:

1. **On Android:** Settings → Google → Manage your Google Account → Data & privacy → **Ads** → turn "Personalized ads" on/off, or reset/delete your Advertising ID.

3. **My Ad Center:** https://myadcenter.google.com to adjust the ads you see.

Turning off personalized ads **does not** remove ads entirely; you may still see non-personalized ads.
